About Leo

Hi, I'm Leo MorshEd.

I'm an AI automation specialist based in Bangladesh, working with businesses and freelancers worldwide. I got into automation after watching teams waste hours every week on tasks a workflow could handle in seconds — copy-pasting leads, manually replying to the same FAQs, chasing follow-ups that fall through the cracks.

Since then I've focused on one thing: building automation systems that actually hold up in production, not just demo well. I work hands-on with Make.com, n8n, Clay, Apollo.io, and GPT-4 to turn manual workflows into systems that run themselves — so my clients get their time back.

How much does a project usually cost?+
It depends on scope — a single workflow starts around ৳5,000, while full systems (chatbots, lead pipelines) run ৳15,000–30,000. See the Pricing section above, or book a discovery call for an exact quote.
How long does a typical build take?+
Simple automations: 2-4 days. Multi-step systems like chatbots or lead-gen pipelines: 1-2 weeks, depending on how many tools need to connect.
What if the automation breaks after delivery?+
Every project includes 7-14 days of free support after handoff. After that, I offer affordable monthly maintenance retainers if you want ongoing peace of mind.
Do I need to already use Make.com, n8n, or other tools?+
No. I'll recommend the right stack for your needs and budget, and set everything up — including new accounts where needed.
How do payments work?+
Typically 50% upfront to start, 50% on delivery. For larger custom projects we can break this into milestones.
